Privacy Policy for Diyo 2048

Last updated: October 2026

What we store. Diyo 2048 saves your best score, your daily streak and your settings (sound, Nepali numbers) only on your own device, using local storage. We do not have accounts and we do not collect your name, email, phone number or location.

Ads. The game may show ads provided by third-party ad networks. These networks may use identifiers such as the advertising ID of your device or cookies to show ads and measure how they perform. Their use of your data follows their own privacy policies.

Children. The game is a general puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13.

Sharing your score. If you tap Share, only the score card text you see is sent, and only to the app you choose.
