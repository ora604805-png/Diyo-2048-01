🪔 How to Play

The goal

  • Merge numbered tiles to make the 2048 tile. Keep going after that for a higher score! लक्ष्य: टाइलहरू जोडेर २०४८ बनाउनुहोस्।

How to move

  1. Swipe up, down, left or right. All tiles slide that way as far as they can. (On a computer, use the arrow keys.) स्वाइप गर्नुहोस् — सबै टाइलहरू त्यतै सर्छन्।
  2. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one. The new tile is the total. एउटै नम्बरका दुई टाइल जोडिएर एउटा हुन्छ।
22 → 4
88 → 16
  1. After every move, a new tile (2 or 4) appears. The board fills up, so merge often! हरेक चाल पछि नयाँ टाइल आउँछ।
  2. Game over when the board is full and no tiles can merge. बोर्ड भरिए र जोड्न नमिले खेल सकिन्छ।

Tips to score high

  • Keep your biggest tile in a corner and don't move it.
  • Try to use only 3 directions. Avoid the 4th unless you have to.
  • Build a row of big tiles in order, like 128, 64, 32, 16.
  • Plan one move ahead before you swipe.

Game modes and buttons

  • Classic: play as long as you like and beat your best score.
  • 📅 Daily Challenge: everyone gets the same tiles today. Play every day to grow your 🔥 streak.
  • Undo (ad): watch a short ad to take back your last move.
  • Game over? Watch an ad to clear 2 small tiles and keep playing.
  • Share: send your score card to friends and challenge them.
  • 🔊 / नेपाली अंक: turn sound on or off, or show Nepali numbers.
